CLOUDBANK PROGRAMS. No cost: NSF sponsors access to commercial cloud resources through ACCESS and the NAIRR Pilot for researchers and educators. Consulting and helpdesk support: Experts are available to help estimate costs, onboard, and troubleshoot issues. Facilitated cloud access and account management: Cloud accounts are provisioned quickly, allow log in to cloud consoles with familiar credentials, and share access with collaborators. Multiple clouds: Researchers can explore and access multiple clouds to find the right services with CloudBank's pay by use charge model. Monitoring and resource usage optimization: Spending is monitored across multiple clouds and triggers automated email balance alerts. Cost optimization recommendations detect unused or underutilized resources. Curated training materials: CloudBank training videos and curated cloud training materials help researchers get started with cloud services.
