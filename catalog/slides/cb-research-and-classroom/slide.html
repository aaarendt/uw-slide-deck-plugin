TWO WAYS TO USE CLOUDBANK. CloudBank has two offerings: one for research projects and one for teaching. CloudBank Research gives a research project its own accounts on the commercial clouds (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, IBM Cloud), paid from an ACCESS or NAIRR Pilot allocation, with CloudBank handling billing, monitoring and support. CloudBank Classroom is a cloud-hosted JupyterHub for teaching. Students log in with their university credentials and work in the browser with no local setup. Each hub comes with Python and R, JupyterLab, RStudio and VS Code, and instructor tools for distributing materials and grading. It is available to U.S.-based educators through an Explore ACCESS allocation, and CloudBank staff set up the course environment. Background you can mention: Classroom is built on the Berkeley Data Stack, the platform behind UC Berkeley's Data 8 course. A partnership with 2i2c has extended it to community colleges that could not run such a system themselves, and work on teaching AI in the classroom has started. The SDSC, UC Berkeley and UW work behind it won the HPCwire 2023 Readers' Choice Award for Best Use of HPC in the Cloud (use case).
