THE FRICTION: WHAT WE'VE HEARD FROM RESEARCHERS. These are paraphrased from conversations with researchers over the years, not verbatim quotes. Let them land before moving on; each one is a problem the following slides answer. Getting an account: grants and university purchasing often don't fit a pay-as-you-go cloud bill, and many researchers stall before they start. CloudBank needs no credit card or purchase agreement. Moving off the laptop: the work runs, just too slowly, and the researcher doesn't know how to move it without starting over. CloudBank's consulting and helpdesk help with exactly this step. Every platform is different: each cloud has its own console, accounts and vocabulary. CloudBank gives one billing account across several clouds. The surprise bill: cloud charges are easy to run up and hard to read. CloudBank monitors spending, sends balance alerts and enforces a spending cap.
