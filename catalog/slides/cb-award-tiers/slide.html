GRANT AWARD TIERS. Explore: up to $10,000; average award $5000; examples: Grad students, first time cloud user, no PI status on any grants. 1 page proposal. Discover: $10,001-$37,500; average award $18,750; examples: Larger multi-user application or scaling an existing experiment. 3 page proposal. Accelerate: $37,501-$75,000; average award $56,250; examples: Established scientific gateway with many users over multiple years. Hundreds of "Explore" awards available per year. Discover & Accelerate awards are competitive (limited number per year and more justification required). Awards provided in 2 installments, renewable if needed after 1 year.
