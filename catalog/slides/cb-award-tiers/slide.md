# Description

A three-row table of award tiers (range, average award, typical use) with three facts below it: Explore awards are plentiful, the larger tiers are competitive, and awards come in two installments.

The on-slide text is condensed to fit the 1.5rem minimum font size. The
original wording is in the speaker notes (presenter view), so the presenter
can quote it exactly.
