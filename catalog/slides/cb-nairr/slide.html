CLOUDBANK ALSO AVAILABLE THROUGH THE NAIRR PILOT. Besides ACCESS, researchers and educators can get a CloudBank account through the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot. How it differs from ACCESS: it is focused on AI research and education. Allocations are only for U.S.-based researchers, and that means everyone on the project, not just the PI. The original awards were in credits; they are now cash awards. Review tends to take longer: requests are reviewed on a monthly cycle, and a request submitted by the 15th is typically decided by the end of the following month, where an ACCESS Explore request can reach a working account in under a week. NAIRR Pilot projects run for 12 months. The portal has three routes: researcher resources (computing, cloud, models, software and platforms; application required), classroom and educator resources (for example CloudBank Classroom JupyterHubs; application required), and data, models and more (freely available, no application). Apply at nairrpilot.org. In September 2026 NSF funded a NAIRR Operations Center, led by SDSC with TACC, to build on the pilot; existing opportunities remain on nairrpilot.org.
