CLOUDBANK ALSO AVAILABLE THROUGH THE NAIRR PILOT. Besides ACCESS, researchers and educators can get a CloudBank account through the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot. How it differs from ACCESS: it is focused on AI research and education. Allocations are only for U.S.-based researchers, and that means everyone on the project, not just the PI. The original awards were in credits; they are now cash awards. Review tends to take longer: requests are reviewed on a monthly cycle, and a request submitted by the 15th is typically decided by the end of the following month, where an ACCESS Explore request can reach a working account in under a week. NAIRR Pilot projects run for 12 months. REQUESTING CLOUDBANK. Prepare: estimate cloud costs with the providers' calculators; have the number of any grant that supports the work; have a CV or resume of up to three pages for the PI and each co-PI; and create an ACCESS account (existing CloudBank users use the same email). Request: submit a research resource request at nairrpilot.org with a three-page proposal and the cost estimate. You do not book CloudBank directly: reviewers and matching committees pair the proposal with resources, and cloud awards on AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud are managed by CloudBank. Onboard: after the award, the PI reviews the CloudBank onboarding presentation, acknowledges the onboarding document and submits a monthly cost estimate in the CloudBank portal; CloudBank then activates the funds and accounts. Aim to finish this within one to two weeks of the award. Collaborators are added through the NAIRR allocations portal. Teaching: a NAIRR Pilot Classroom request (course title and abstract, syllabus, number of students, class dates) gets a CloudBank-hosted JupyterHub for the course; allocations last a year and can be renewed. Data, pre-trained models and some other NAIRR resources need no application at all. In September 2026 NSF funded a NAIRR Operations Center, led by SDSC with TACC, to build on the pilot; existing opportunities remain on nairrpilot.org.
