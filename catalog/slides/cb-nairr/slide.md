# Description

The second route to a CloudBank account, the NAIRR Pilot, and how it
differs from ACCESS. Meant to follow `cb-process` (the ACCESS route) or
`cb-research-and-classroom`.

The left column lists four differences from ACCESS as labelled rows (focus,
who, awards, review). The right column gives the three numbered steps to
request CloudBank through the NAIRR Pilot (prepare, request, onboard), with
what each needs, and a shaded line pointing educators to NAIRR Classroom
projects. The portal address is linked in the Request step. The NSF portal
screenshot on the source slide is not included, so the slide carries no
third-party artwork.

The on-slide text is condensed to fit the 1.5rem minimum font size. The
speaker notes give the full detail of each step, the Classroom request, the
12-month project length and the 2026 NAIRR Operations Center award.

## Sources

Adapted from a CloudBank slide supplied by the slide owner on 2026-11-03.
Checked against the pages below on 2026-11-03.

- "AI focus", "U.S.-based", the monthly review cycle and the 12-month
  project length: NAIRR Pilot resource requests page.
- The three request steps: CloudBank's NAIRR Pilot Research guide (cost
  estimate, grant number, CVs of up to three pages for PI and co-PIs, ACCESS
  account), the NAIRR resource requests page (three-page proposal, matching
  committees) and the NAIRR knowledge base's CloudBank onboarding page
  (onboarding presentation, acknowledgement, monthly cost estimate, 1-2 weeks).
- The Classroom line: CloudBank's NAIRR Pilot Classroom guide.
- "Everyone on the project, not just the lead": from the slide owner; the
  NAIRR Pilot pages do not state it.
- "Early awards were in credits, now cash awards": from the slide owner. The
  ACCESS CloudBank Research page states that CloudBank awards are made in
  dollars, and the owner's "CloudBank Allocations (Oct'25 - Oct'26)" report
  (2026-11-03) records about 20 NAIRR Pilot awards transitioned after vendor
  credits expired on 2026-06-30.
- The ACCESS comparison ("under a week") comes from `cb-process`.

The NAIRR Pilot pages now say "SI" (super intelligence) where they used to
say "AI". This slide keeps "AI" throughout, by the slide owner's decision on
2026-11-03; do not switch it when re-checking against those pages.

Re-check when NAIRR moves from the pilot to the NAIRR Operations Center
(awarded September 2026): the portal address and the word "Pilot" may change.
Bump `version` whenever the wording changes.
