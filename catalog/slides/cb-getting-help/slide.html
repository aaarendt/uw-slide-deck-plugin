LEARNING AND SUPPORT. You don't have to figure out cloud billing and infrastructure alone; CloudBank has help at every level, from videos you watch on your own to a person who answers email. Learn on your own. Video tutorials at cloudbank.org/video-tutorials, all on CloudBank's YouTube channel: Cloud 101 is six short fundamentals videos (thinking cloudily, managing costs, cloud services, collaboration, security, scale); Cloud Clinics are topical deep dives, a recorded talk with live Q&A, about one a month, with past topics such as checkpointing, AWS capacity blocks, AI cloud services, SkyPilot, serverless databases and teaching with CloudBank Classroom. Pick a topic and watch it when it becomes relevant. The CloudBank Catalog is an at-a-glance comparison of compute, storage, network, database and analytics services across the public clouds offered through CloudBank, with links to each provider's full service list. Ask a person. Office hours are a one-hour Zoom session once a month, 10 to 11 am Pacific, run by the CloudBank team from UC San Diego and the University of Washington. They are open to anyone new to the program and to current users with an operational or technical problem: bring a draft proposal, a billing question, or just "I'm not sure where to start." The Zoom link is on each session's page on cloudbank.org and on the ACCESS events calendar. The community forum at community.cloudbank.org is for questions that don't fit in office hours; search it first, since someone may have asked already. Write to us. For anything else, or anything about your own account, email help@cloudbank.org.
