WHAT CLOUDBANK IS. NSF's managed gateway to commercial cloud for research. Since 2019: NSF-funded cloud access entity, co-led by UC San Diego (SDSC), UC Berkeley, and UW eScience. Cloud providers: Managed access to AWS, Google Cloud, IBM Cloud, and Microsoft Azure, plus onboarding, training, billing support, and a shared portal. CloudBank 2.0: $20M NSF award in 2025 — five-year horizon, ~500 projects/year, expanded eligibility to any U.S. researcher or educator at academic, non-profit research, or educational institutions. NAIRR interface: CloudBank is the interface for private-sector cloud contributions to the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot.
