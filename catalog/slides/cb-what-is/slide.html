WHAT CLOUDBANK IS. CloudBank is a managed service that simplifies access to commercial cloud. It is not a cloud itself: your work runs on the commercial clouds, and CloudBank handles the accounts and the money. Researcher: a researcher or educator with an allocation from ACCESS or the NAIRR Pilot. CloudBank: after onboarding, the CloudBank portal creates your accounts on the clouds you asked for, lets you sign in with your ACCESS credentials and choose who can use them, applies the charges against your award, and sends spending alerts, so there is no credit card and one bill across clouds. The commercial clouds: AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure and IBM Cloud, plus onboarding, training, billing support and a shared portal from CloudBank. [PENDING: the CloudBank Catalog also lists Oracle Cloud Infrastructure; confirm whether to show four clouds or five.] Who runs it: an NSF-funded cloud access entity since 2019, co-led by UC San Diego (SDSC), UC Berkeley and UW eScience. CloudBank 2.0: $20M NSF award in 2025, a five-year horizon, ~500 projects a year, and eligibility expanded to any U.S. researcher or educator at academic, non-profit research, or educational institutions. NAIRR: CloudBank is the interface for private-sector cloud contributions to the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot.
