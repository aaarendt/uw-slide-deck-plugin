WHAT CLOUDBANK IS. NSF's managed gateway to commercial cloud for research. CloudBank is not a cloud itself; it sits between you and the commercial clouds. You: a researcher or educator with an allocation from ACCESS or the NAIRR Pilot. CloudBank: after onboarding, the CloudBank portal creates your accounts on the clouds you asked for, lets you sign in with your ACCESS credentials and choose who can use them, applies the charges against your award, and sends spending alerts, so there is no credit card and one bill across clouds. The clouds: managed access to AWS, Google Cloud, IBM Cloud and Microsoft Azure, plus onboarding, training, billing support and a shared portal. [PENDING: the CloudBank Catalog also lists Oracle Cloud Infrastructure; confirm whether to show four clouds or five.] Since 2019: NSF-funded cloud access entity, co-led by UC San Diego (SDSC), UC Berkeley, and UW eScience. CloudBank 2.0: $20M NSF award in 2025, a five-year horizon, ~500 projects a year, and eligibility expanded to any U.S. researcher or educator at academic, non-profit research, or educational institutions. NAIRR: CloudBank is the interface for private-sector cloud contributions to the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot.
