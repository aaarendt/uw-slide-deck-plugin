Eligibility and recommended uses

Who
U.S.-based researchers and educators, graduate student or above
What
Projects with a scientific focus, or that support a scientific study

Good fit

  • Modest-scale AI workloads
  • Testing software scalability
  • Unique hardware or cloud services
  • Distributed-cloud and network studies
  • Hosting tools, datasets and gateways

Better elsewhere

  • Large-scale compute
  • GPU-intensive workloads

Use on-premises ACCESS or NAIRR resources instead

Sources: ACCESS allocations policy · CloudBank recommended uses

ACCESS eligibility, verbatim: "To qualify to lead an allocated project, you must be a U.S.-based researcher or educator, at the graduate-student level or higher." CloudBank recommended uses, verbatim: the application must have a scientific focus or support a scientific study; AI workloads that are modest in scale; validating and testing the scalability of software tools or systems; accessing unique hardware or specialized cloud tools and services; studying the behavior of regionally or globally distributed cloud environments, including network performance; hosting community tools, datasets, or science gateways. CloudBank is not intended to support large-scale compute or GPU-intensive workloads, which can be executed more cost-effectively on other on-premises ACCESS/NAIRR resources.
