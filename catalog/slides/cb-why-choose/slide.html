WHAT YOU GET WITH A CLOUDBANK ACCOUNT. Billing, handled for you. No credit card or university purchase agreement: NSF sponsors commercial cloud access through ACCESS and the NAIRR Pilot, and your award pays for it. One billing account across several clouds: AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure and IBM Cloud. [PENDING: the CloudBank Catalog also lists Oracle Cloud Infrastructure; confirm whether there are four clouds or five before naming them on the slide.] A spending cap: CloudBank monitors spending across all your clouds, sends automated balance alerts, and recommends shutting down unused or underused resources. [PENDING: what happens to running resources when the cap is reached, and to stored data when the allocation ends.] Dollars, not credits: awards are grant dollars, not vendor credits, so they also pay for marketplace services: third-party software and AI model access. More than virtual machines. Shared access: accounts are set up for you after onboarding, you log in to each cloud with your ACCESS credentials, and you choose which collaborators can use them. Move big datasets: Globus moves large datasets reliably between campus storage, clusters and cloud storage, restarting after network drops and checking every file. Use AI models: hosted model services such as Amazon Bedrock, Azure AI and Google Vertex AI let your code call large language models through an API and pay per use, without setting up GPUs; you can also run your own model on a hosted endpoint. Host a tool or dataset: CloudBank supports science gateways, with community accounts and hosting for data collections, databases, services and web interfaces.
