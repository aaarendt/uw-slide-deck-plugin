WHAT YOU GET WITH A CLOUDBANK ACCOUNT. Billing, handled for you. No credit card or university purchase agreement: NSF sponsors commercial cloud access through ACCESS and the NAIRR Pilot, and your award pays for it. One billing account across several clouds: AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure and IBM Cloud. [PENDING: the CloudBank Catalog also lists Oracle Cloud Infrastructure; confirm whether there are four clouds or five before naming them on the slide.] A spending cap: CloudBank monitors spending across all your clouds, sends automated balance alerts, and recommends shutting down unused or underused resources. [PENDING: what happens to running resources when the cap is reached, and to stored data when the allocation ends.] Dollars, not credits: awards are grant dollars, not vendor credits, so they also pay for marketplace services: third-party software and AI model access. Accounts, handled for you. Set up for you: after you finish onboarding, the CloudBank portal creates accounts on each cloud in your request. One login: you sign in to each cloud's own console with your ACCESS credentials. Collaborators: you decide in the CloudBank portal which authorized ACCESS users can use each cloud account. Room to grow: you can add another cloud to the project later. CloudBank also offers Globus for moving large datasets between campus and cloud storage; help and training are on the getting help slide.
